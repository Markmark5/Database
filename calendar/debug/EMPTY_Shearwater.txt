Business Highlights
What we’ve achieved this year
1
Strong growth in revenue and adjusted EBITDA
Revenue of £39.5m for the 15 months to 30 June 2025 represented an annualised increase of 29%, while adjusted EBITDA of £2.2m reflected annualised growth of 91%.
2
Delivered a number of major, multi-year contract wins with both new and existing clients. Major contracts wins in the period included:
£4.4m three-year data-security contract, representing expansion of scope with existing client
£8.4m two-year renewal with long-standing client
$12.8m five-year contract with new client
3
Continued to operate at the cutting-edge of new technologies, including AI
Launched own proprietary AI solution and became only second organisation in the UK to receive the pioneering ISO42001 standard around use of AI
4
Significantly enhanced the capabilities of our software portfolio
Rolled out a number of features during the period, to significantly improve the functionality and level of differentiation of our proprietary software in relation to its competitors.
