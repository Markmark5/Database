Nexteq is listed on the London Stock Exchange on the Alternative Investment Market (AIM:NXQ)
Nexteq’s headquarters are located in Crawley in the UK, with subsidiary companies located in the US, Italy, Germany, France, Taiwan and Japan. The business trades with customers in nearly 50 countries, with the US representing the Group’s largest market.
On the Investor Hub you’ll find all the latest corporate, financial and shareholder information for Nexteq. The information disclosed here is designed to comply with the requirements of Rule 26 of the AIM Rules for Companies – Company Information Disclosure, and was last updated on 30 June 2026.
