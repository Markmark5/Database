Dividend
Information
The Board is not proposing any final dividend payment for the year ended 31 March 2026 (2025: $nil). The Group maintains a clear capital allocation discipline and remains committed to returning excess funds to shareholders over time, whether through future dividends and/or share repurchase programmes, as appropriate.
The Company has established an EBT in respect of which all employees of the Group, including Executive Directors, are potential beneficiaries.
At 31 March 2026, the EBT held 413,706 shares in the Company (2025: 417,307 shares). It is expected that the EBT will acquire additional shares in the year to 31 March 2027 in anticipation of future settlement of awards granted under the Company’s employee share schemes, namely the Restricted Share Plan (RSP), the Annual Performance Bonus Plan (APBP), and the Value Creation Plan (VCP).
It is anticipated that the EBT will waive any dividend rights attaching to shares held in the EBT.
