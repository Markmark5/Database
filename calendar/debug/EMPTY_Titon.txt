Shareholder information
Welcome to the Titon Holdings Plc website and the shareholder information section. You will find contact details for the Company Secretary and Company Registrar below.
We welcome your involvement with Titon: we look forward to meeting you at the Annual General Meeting in March of each year. We are also keen to answer any questions you may have about Titon during the year by contacting us.
