Welcome to Marston’s Investor Relations
We are a leading local pub business with an estate of more than 1,300 pubs, supported by c.9,000 employees, and our vision is to be the UK’s leading local pub company. Our Purpose is to offer our guests the best experience and locations for Shared Good Times.
This is underpinned by a clear strategy to create a high-margin, highly cash-generative model based on differentiated formats and a brand portfolio that is naturally balanced to appeal across a range of consumers.
