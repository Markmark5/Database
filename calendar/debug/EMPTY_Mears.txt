Investor FAQ
Investor Centre login
Where is the Mears Group listed?
Mears Group PLC (MER) is fully listed on the London Stock Exchange.
Where is the price of our shares quoted on the Stock Exchange?
The FTSE ticker for Mears Group is MER.L. The share price is available on our
investor website
.
How will I get paid my dividends?
Your Mears Group PLC dividends will be automatically paid to you by cheque or alternatively, we would recommend that you opt to receive your dividends directly into your bank account. This will ensure that you receive your dividend on the payment date and avoids the inconvenience of depositing a cheque. Your bank details can be registered through the Investor Centre. Sign in or register and select "View/Amend my bank details".
I have changed my email address, how do I notify you?
If you wish to change the email address that you have registered with us for electronic communications, sign in or register at Investor Centre. Once logged in, select the tab 'My Profile' followed by the option 'Communications Preferences'. Then follow the onscreen instructions.
If you require to change the email address you have registered to receive
Investor Centre
communications, select the tab 'My Profile' followed by the option 'Member Details'.
I have changed my address, how do I notify you?
If you need to update the registered address on your shareholding, the most secure and environmentally friendly approach is to do this online, where you can perform a range of self service options across your portfolio and manage your shareholding online.
Sign in or register at
Investor Centre
. Once logged in select the tab 'My Profile' followed by the option 'Address Change'.
Please be advised that it is NOT necessary to return your share certificates for alteration for change of address. Please note neither Mears nor Computershare can accept change of address by fax or by email.
How do I sell my Mears Group PLC shares?
You may use a share dealing service such as those offered by High Street Banks or Stockbrokers. Additionally, Computershare Investor Services PLC can offer you access to a share dealing service. You can buy or sell shares in certificated format and dispose of or increase holdings that are held in a Computershare nominee account.
Sign in or register at
Investor Centre
. Once logged in you will be able to see the options available to you for buying or selling shares.
I have lost my share certificate. What should I do?
Replacement share certificate(s) can be obtained through the completion and return of an Indemnity for lost certificate(s). In most instances it is usual for an administration fee to be charged for the production of an Indemnity. Original certificates are cancelled when an Indemnity is raised to cover the risks associated with their loss so please ensure that you are certain that the certificate has been lost before reporting it so, as fees may be incurred. Please call our registrars, Computershare, to discuss your circumstances on +44 (0370) 889 3192.
